Support

Something not working, a card missing, or an idea for the app? Here is how to get in touch.

{{icon:lifebuoy}}

The best way to reach me is GitHub. Open an issue with your question or the problem you ran into, and I'll answer there. You can also check existing issues first. Posting needs a free GitHub account.

What helps me fix a problem

  • Your device and system version, for example "Galaxy Z Fold 6, Android 16" or "iPhone, iOS 27".
  • The app version (on Android it is shown in Settings).
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot helps.
  • For a card that scans wrong, the code printed in its bottom-left corner, for example OGN • 066a/298 • EN.

Common questions

A card is scanned as the wrong version.

Tap the set and number chip under the scanned card to see every printing of that card, then pick the one you have. A chip marked "?" means the app could not tell the versions apart on its own.

The language detected is wrong.

Tap the flag chip under the scanned card to change it, or choose a fixed language instead of Auto for the next scans.

A card shows as banned but isn't in my region.

Bans can differ by competitive region. Choose your region in Settings; the app updates the ban list right away.

A new set or a promo card is missing.

The app updates its card list on launch whenever new data is available. If a card is still missing, open an issue with its name and code.

How do I move my collection to a new phone?

Use Settings to export a backup file, then restore it on the new device. The same file works on Android and iOS.

Privacy

ScanRift has no account and collects no personal data. Read the privacy policy.

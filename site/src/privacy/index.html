Privacy policy

Applies to the ScanRift apps for Android and iOS. Last updated 14 November 2026.

{{icon:shield-check}}

In short: ScanRift does not collect, sell or share personal data. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics. Your collection stays on your device.

Camera

ScanRift asks for camera access to read the collector number printed on your cards. Camera frames and photos are processed on your device by the operating system's text recognition (Apple Vision on iOS, Google ML Kit on Android). They are never uploaded, stored on a server or shared. Photos you pick from your library for a binder-page scan are handled the same way.

Your collection, decks and games

Everything you record in the app (owned cards, their language and condition, lists, decks and game results) is stored in the app's private storage on your device. We have no copy of it.

  • Backups you make. When you export a backup or a collection file, it is written where you choose, for example your files or a cloud drive you control.
  • Android. Android's built-in backup may include the app's data in your personal Google account backup, under Google's own terms. You can turn this off in your device settings.
  • iOS. If you turn on iCloud sync in the app, your data is synced through your own private iCloud account under Apple's terms. It is off unless you enable it.

Card data downloads

The app downloads the public Riftbound card catalogue (card names, images, bans and errata) from our server and from Riot Games' image servers. These are ordinary web requests: like any website, the servers receive your device's IP address and basic request information in order to answer. We do not use them to identify you, we do not combine them with anything else and we do not keep them beyond the hosting provider's standard short-lived logs. Our server is hosted by Cloudflare.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No sale or sharing of data with third parties.

Permissions

  • Camera: to scan cards, as described above.
  • Internet: to download the card catalogue and card images.
  • Vibration: optional feedback when a card is recognised.

Children

ScanRift is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your device. Backup files you exported stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions about privacy can be asked on the support page.

Scan your Riftbound cards. Keep score at the table.

ScanRift is a free app for Riftbound players. Point your phone at your cards to add them to your collection, then use it to count points when you sit down to play.

{{icon:android-logo}} Android available {{icon:apple-logo}} iPhone and iPad coming soon

What you can do with it

  • {{icon:scan}}

    Scan your cards

    Hold a card under the camera and ScanRift reads the number in the corner. Got a full binder page? One photo picks up every card on it. If you own an alternate art or a signed copy, you choose the exact version in one tap.

  • {{icon:books}}

    Track your collection

    See what you own and what you're still missing, set by set. English, French, Chinese and Korean copies are kept apart, so you know exactly which one sits in your binder.

  • {{icon:trophy}}

    Count points

    Lay your phone on the table and tap your side to score. Note whether the point came from conquering, holding or an ability, and take it back if you misclick. For 2 to 4 players, with best-of-three matches and a game history.

  • {{icon:cards}}

    Build decks

    Put a deck together from your cards or paste a list from riftbound.gg. ScanRift checks the rules as you go and warns you when a card is banned or has been errata'd.

  • {{icon:columns}}

    Comfortable on big screens

    On a foldable or a tablet, the list and the card sit side by side, and you can drag the divider to the width you like.

  • {{icon:shield-check}}

    Nothing to sign up for

    No account, no ads, no tracking. Your collection stays on your phone. Privacy policy.

Questions

Is ScanRift free?

Yes. There are no ads, no subscription and no account to create.

Which card languages does it handle?

English, French, Chinese and Korean. The language is picked up while you scan, and you can always change it by hand.

Does it work with the newest set?

Yes. The card list updates by itself when you open the app, and Radiance is already in.

Is there an iPhone version?

It's on its way. The Android version is available now.

Open source

The Android app is built in the open. You can read the code, follow what's coming next or report a bug on GitHub.

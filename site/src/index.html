Your Riftbound collection, one scan away.

ScanRift is a companion app for the Riftbound trading card game. Point your camera at a card or a whole binder page, and it lands in your collection, ready for deck building.

{{icon:android-logo}} Android available {{icon:apple-logo}} iPhone and iPad coming soon

What it does

  • {{icon:scan}}

    Scan cards

    Reads the collector number with the camera, one card at a time or a full binder page from a single photo. Pick the exact version (alternate art, overnumbered, signature) in one tap.

  • {{icon:translate}}

    Every language

    Keeps English, French, Chinese and Korean copies apart, detects the language while scanning and sorts your collection by flag.

  • {{icon:cards}}

    Build decks

    Checks the deck rules as you build, flags banned cards for your competitive region and shows the official errata on each card.

  • {{icon:trophy}}

    Track games

    A point tracker for two to four players, with best-of-three matches and a history of your results.

  • {{icon:columns}}

    Made for big screens

    Two panes on foldables and tablets, with a divider you can drag to the width you like.

  • {{icon:shield-check}}

    No account, no tracking

    Your collection stays on your device. No sign-up, no ads, no analytics. Privacy policy.

Open source

The Android app is developed in the open. Read the code, follow its progress or report a problem on GitHub.
